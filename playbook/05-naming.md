# Naming: why the app is FoodTrip

Checked 2026-11-11. Domains by RDAP/whois (registered or not; a premium price can still apply at checkout).
App Store by the iTunes Search API, US store.

## Decision

**FoodTrip**, at **getfoodtrip.app** (Nick bought it 2026-11-11). Bundle ID `app.getfoodtrip.foodtrip`, the same
pattern as Good Walk (getgoodwalk.app).

The first idea was FoodPlan. It was dropped because "food plan" means meal prep and dieting everywhere it appears
(Samsung Food, MealPrepPro, Eat This Much), there is already an app called Foodplan, and foodplan.com/.app/.co are all
registered. FoodTrip says what the app does: a trip planned around food.

App Store: "Food Trip" (Manish Chak, no ratings), "FoodtripPH", "K-Food Trip", "Food Trip Merge" (a game). None is a
restaurant itinerary planner, and none has a meaningful audience. An App Store name must be unique, so list it as
**"FoodTrip: Restaurant Trip Planner"** (30 characters is the limit; that is 32, so use
**"FoodTrip: Eat Trip Planner"** or **"FoodTrip - Restaurant Trips"**, both under 30). Check the name in App Store
Connect before the listing copy is written: the search API does not show reserved names.

Not checked: trademarks. Search "FOODTRIP" at USPTO (tmsearch.uspto.gov) in classes 9 and 39/43 before spending on the brand.

## FoodPlan (rejected)

foodplan.com, foodplan.app, foodplan.co, foodplanapp.com, getfoodplan.com: all registered. getfoodplan.app and
foodplanapp.app: not registered. App Store: "Foodplan" (Aleksei Vinokurov), "EASYFOODPLAN", "Faustformel Foodplan".
