How does FoodTrip choose restaurants?

Every place holds a MICHELIN Guide distinction or a James Beard Foundation honor, or was checked open by hand for Eats Ranked. Chains are left out, and no restaurant pays to be listed. For each day FoodTrip picks dinner first, then lunch, breakfast and an afternoon treat, keeping to your budget, using a different cuisine each meal and keeping the day's stops close together.

Does FoodTrip book the table?

No. Reserve opens OpenTable's search for that restaurant with your date, time and party size filled in. If the restaurant isn't on OpenTable, "Other ways to book" searches for its own booking page or phone number. The reservation is between you and the restaurant.

Are the prices and times exact?

No. Prices are estimates per person for food, tax and tip, without drinks. Times are suggestions (9:00, 12:30, 3:30 and 7:00). Check the restaurant's hours, book the time you can get, then edit the calendar event to match.

A meal is missing, or a day is over budget

Smaller cities have fewer picks. On a long trip the cheaper places can run out, so a late day may say how far over budget it is, or leave a meal out. A higher budget, fewer meals a day or a shorter trip fixes it.

Changing the plan

Swap replaces one stop with the next best and leaves the rest alone. Shuffle makes a new plan from the same choices.

Calendar

On iPhone, FoodTrip asks for permission to add events only; it can't read your calendar. If you declined, turn it on in Settings → Apps → FoodTrip → Calendars. Events are saved at the city's local time. On the website, "Add to calendar" downloads a calendar file that Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook can open.

Price, and restoring a purchase

The website is free. In the iPhone app your first 5 trips are free; after that a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited trips. There is no subscription. Trips you already planned stay open either way. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, tap "Restore purchase" on the unlock screen while signed in to the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My city isn't listed

FoodTrip lists a city once it has at least 20 picks there. More are added as Eats Ranked covers more places. Email us the city you want.
