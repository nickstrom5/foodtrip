Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

FoodTrip collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, on the website or in the iPhone app.

Your trip

The city, dates, meals, budget and party size you choose are used on your device to build the plan. On the website they are kept in the page's address so that you can reopen or share the plan; they are not sent to us. In the iPhone app, saved trips are stored on your phone.

Calendar

When you add meals to your calendar, the iPhone app asks for permission to add events only. It cannot read your calendar. The website gives you a calendar file or opens Google Calendar; nothing is sent to us.

Reservations, maps and other links

Reserve, Directions and Google Calendar open another company's site or app (OpenTable, Google, Apple Maps) with the restaurant's name and, for a reservation, your date, time and party size. What happens there is covered by that company's privacy policy. FoodTrip is not affiliated with them and earns nothing from these links.

Purchases

The website is free. In the iPhone app, the first five trips are free and a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited trips. Apple handles the purchase; FoodTrip never sees your payment details, and learns only whether the purchase exists.
